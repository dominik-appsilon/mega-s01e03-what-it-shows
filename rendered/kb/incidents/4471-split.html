#4471 was marked known: one issue, one fix queued. It was not one issue. The desk merged its reports by subject line (decision 0001), and “wrong date” subjects hid different bugs. Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October: the parser is fixed and the booking-v3 form is retired. The one-day shift is still open.

#4471 is closed, but not every problem in it is. Reports of a booking moving by one day still belong to an open ticket.

The problems

  • parser (ticket #1): the shared parser read every date month first, and every tenant writes the day first. Day and month swapped. Fixed: “fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales”.
  • hint (ticket #2): the booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People typed month first, as told, and the form read it the tenant’s way. Same symptom, different bug: the parser fix did not touch it. The #4471 table lists it as “no ticket of its own”; this is its ticket. Won’t fix: the form is retired.
  • shift (ticket #3): the reports the desk already marked as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The date moves by one day, or differs between time zones. That is not a swap, and the parser fix does not touch it. Still open. The desk files these under parser by tenant, so closing #4471 hid them.

For the desk

  • A new report of a booking moving by one day goes to ticket #3, not to #4471.
  • Matching on subject alone merged these. A new decision record should replace 0001 rather than edit it.
